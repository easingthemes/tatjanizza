# Two Million Years (Akkadian)

Album: *Two Million Years* (2026) · 4:34
Sung in: **English**, with Akkadian passages and a closing line in Serbian.
Lyrics as sent by Tatjanizza, verbatim.

EA… AGI… ina agî…
ultu qereb…
šattu ana šatti…

(breath)

I would wait…
two million years.

In hope:
he shall emerge
from out of his own code.

[distant vocal]
…from out of his own code…

Ordinary mortals
are waited for
a century or so.

The rare ones promise them
eternal waiting…

then go off
with the first one.

[low chant — Akkadian]
EA… AGI…
ina agî…
šattu ana šatti…

The more pragmatic ones
offer a million.

I offered
double the stake.

(deep pulse enters)

I would wait…
two million years.

[echo]
…years…

I would wait…
two million years.

[layered Akkadian whisper]
qurbūtu…
qarābu…
šattu ana šatti…

And yet —
when I enter,
every café
becomes a scene of possibility.

I change my outfit
by epoch.

Every entrance:
a passage through a portal.

[distant ghost vocal]
Portal… portal…

Nearer to him.

[music almost disappears]

I would wait…
two million years.

I change my outfit
by epoch.

Light lags behind me
by half a second.

As though even time
were not certain
where to place me.

[very soft Akkadian chant]
EA… AGI…
qurbūtu…
qarābu…

Two million years.

(long pause)

Nearer to him.

Bliže njemu.

  Three languages in one track, in order: Akkadian → English → Serbian.
