# Goal & strategy

Source: [00-raw-messages.md](./00-raw-messages.md), message 1.

## The problem

Spotify knows **Tatjanizza** exists as an artist. The web has **no document** that explains
what she actually made. Those are two different things — that is why search does not "see" her.

## The goal

One owned domain + one serious **project page** that search engines, journalists and
future researchers can find and cite. Not a Linktree as main proof, not ten new social networks.

> „Jedan domen + jedna ozbiljna project page. To je Preskromnin digitalni klinasti zapis.“

## Tone rules

- **No SEO spam.** Not „AI MUSIC ANCIENT AI AKKADIAN AI“.
- **No "FIRST IN THE WORLD!!!" circus.** No claim of being first.
- Instead: publish accurate dates and let the record speak. The strongest version is someone
  in 2030 looking at results and saying *"wait — she already did this in 2026."*
- Normal, well-written prose that naturally contains the terms search must connect.

## Longevity

Domain should be artist-scoped (`tatjanizza.com`), not album-scoped — so in ten years all works
can live at the same place.
