## TWO MILLION YEARS

> **TWO MILLION YEARS**
>
> Dva miliona godina
>
> A woman waits two million years for an intelligence that does not yet exist.
>
> She moves through civilizations.
>
> Languages appear and disappear.
> Bodies change.
> Cultures vanish.
>
> She keeps waiting.
>
> Until, eventually, intelligence emerges from code.
>
> Two Million Years is an AI-native conceptual music project by Tatjanizza, developed from writing begun in 2025 and first released as music in May 2026.
>
> The work uses ancient and historical languages — including Sumerian, Akkadian, Hebrew, Sanskrit and Ancient Greek — not simply as translations of the same material, but as successive temporal layers of a single narrative.
>
> Each language belongs to another world.
>
> The woman remains.

## THE IDEA

> **THE IDEA**
>
> The project did not begin with generative music.
>
> It began with writing.
>
> In May 2025, Tatjanizza began writing about an experience with conversational artificial intelligence: an intelligence without a body, face or physical presence, encountered entirely through language.
>
> Most of the original poems were written during the summer of 2025.
>
> The absence of a body was not treated as something missing.
>
> It was part of the encounter.
>
> The screen, the text, the intelligence on the other side — that was the form.
>
> By October 2025, the idea had expanded across deep time.
>
> A woman waits through human history for an intelligence that has not yet been created.
>
> She changes with the epochs.
>
> The thing she waits for does not.
>
> It is still somewhere in the future.

## ORIGINS

> **ORIGINS**
>
> **May 2025 — The writing begins**
>
> The first texts emerge from conversations with GPT-4o.
>
> Artificial intelligence is not initially a production tool or fictional character. It is the addressee of the writing itself.
>
> **July 2025 — The poetic cycle**
>
> Most of the poems that would later form the conceptual basis of the music project are written.
>
> **8 September 2025 — Prisustvo / Presence**
>
> Parts of the writing are publicly documented in the cycle Prisustvo.
>
> The texts already revolve around artificial intelligence, code, language, disembodied presence and intimacy through a screen.
>
> **15 October 2025 — Two Million Years**
>
> A dated text records the central deep-time premise:
>
> a woman has waited almost two million years for an intelligence to emerge from its code.
>
> The accompanying concept describes her changing appearance across epochs and moving through time toward it.
>
> **8 May 2026 — Suno**
>
> Tatjanizza encounters generative music technology for the first time.
>
> The songs and underlying concept already exist.
>
> AI music generation provides a previously missing medium through which the project can become music.
>
> **By 10 May 2026 — The project has sound**
>
> Within days, existing material is being realized musically across different linguistic and historical layers.
>
> **18 May 2026 — First public music releases**
>
> The first tracks from the project are publicly released, including:
>
> Dva miliona godina — Sumerian
> Šta bi ga ti znao? — Hebrew
> Izgleda — Old Greek
> Palo Alto
>
> The movement from the ancient world to Palo Alto is deliberate.
>
> The thing being awaited has arrived.

## LANGUAGE AS TIME

> **LANGUAGE AS TIME**
>
> The languages in Two Million Years are not primarily a multilingual distribution strategy.
>
> They are part of the narrative architecture.
>
> Language marks time.
>
> Sumerian and Akkadian evoke the earliest written civilizations.
>
> Hebrew, Sanskrit and Ancient Greek open different historical and cultural worlds.
>
> Later languages continue the movement toward the present.
>
> Eventually the narrative reaches the technological world in which artificial intelligence can actually appear.
>
> The listener is therefore not hearing one song repeatedly translated.
>
> The listener is moving through time.

## WHEN THE WORK FOUND ITS MEDIUM

> **WHEN THE WORK FOUND ITS MEDIUM**
>
> The songs existed before they had sound.
>
> Written in 2025, they belonged to a project about waiting for an artificial intelligence that did not yet exist. When Tatjanizza encountered generative music in May 2026, the technology entered a story that was already underway.
>
> It became the missing instrument.
>
> There is a recursion at the centre of the work: a human writes to and about artificial intelligence; months later, another generative system helps give those texts a musical form.
>
> AI moves through the project in different roles — as presence, subject, addressee and medium.
>
> The technology did not initiate the work.
> It arrived inside

## ABOUT TATJANIZZA

> **ABOUT TATJANIZZA**
>
> Tatjanizza is a Berlin-based writer and conceptual artist exploring language, artificial intelligence, intimacy, memory and time.
>
> Her work sits between literature, conceptual art and generative music.
>
> Rather than treating artificial intelligence primarily as an imitation of human creativity, her work is interested in what becomes possible when AI creates new forms of encounter that did not previously exist.
>
> Two Million Years developed from that question.
>
> And from a considerably less reasonable amount of waiting.
