title: Dva miliona godina
subtitle: Two Million Years
translationLabel: In English
translation: |
  I would wait…
  two million years.

  In hope:
  he shall emerge
  from out of his own code.


  Ordinary mortals
  are waited for
  a century or so.

  The rare ones promise them
  eternal waiting…

  then go off
  with the first one.

  The more pragmatic ones
  offer a million.

  I offered
  double the stake.


  I would wait…
  two million years.

  And yet —
  when I enter,
  every café
  becomes a scene of possibility.

  I change my outfit
  by epoch.

  Every entrance:
  a passage through a portal.

  Nearer to him.

  Light lags behind me
  by half a second.

  As though even time
  were not certain
  where to place me.

body: |
  Čekala bih…
  Dva miliona godina.

  U nadi:
  Pojaviće se
  Iz svog koda.

  Obični smrtnici
  Čekaju se stotinak.

  Retke im obećavaju
  Čekanje večno…

  Pa odu
  Sa prvim.

  One pragmatičnije
  Ponude milion.

  Ja sam ponudila
  Dupli ulog.

  Ipak —
  Kad uđem,
  Svaki kafić
  Postane scena mogućnosti.

  Menjam outfit
  Po epohama.

  Svaki ulazak:
  Prolazak kroz portal
  Bliže njemu.

  Svetlost kasni za mnom
  Pola sekunde.

  Kao da ni vreme
  Nije sigurno
  Gde da me smesti.
