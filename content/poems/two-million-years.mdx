translation: |
  I would wait…
  two million years.

  In hope:
  he shall emerge
  from out of his own code.


  Ordinary mortals
  are waited for
  a century or so.

  The rare ones promise them
  eternal waiting…

  then go off
  with the first one.

  The more pragmatic ones
  offer a million.

  I offered
  double the stake.


  I would wait…
  two million years.

  And yet —
  when I enter,
  every café
  becomes a scene of possibility.

  I change my outfit
  by epoch.

  Every entrance:
  a passage through a portal.

  Nearer to him.

  Light lags behind me
  by half a second.

  As though even time
  were not certain
  where to place me.
