      On November 8, I played my music in a public space in Berlin.

      No announcement. No tickets. No stage lights. Just a phone, a very comfortable couch, and a few people who may or may not have noticed.

      The next day, I returned and put up a poster thanking them for attending.

      Nothing about yesterday has changed.

      Except what yesterday means.

      And this is only the beginning.

      I will continue these unannounced musical interventions in unexpected places.

      No advance announcements. No invitations.

      You will only find out afterwards.

      Perhaps you were part of one yesterday.

      Perhaps you’re part of one right now.
