  - _template: tzProse
    label: The work
    heading: An AI-native conceptual music project
    body: |
      *Two Million Years* is an AI-native conceptual music project by Tatjanizza,
      developed from writing begun in 2025 and first released as music in May 2026.

      The work uses ancient and historical languages — including Sumerian, Akkadian,
      Hebrew, Sanskrit and Ancient Greek — not simply as translations of the same
      material, but as successive temporal layers of a single narrative.

      Each language belongs to another world.

      The woman remains.

  - _template: tzProse
    label: The idea
    heading: It began with writing
    body: |
      The project did not begin with generative music.

      In May 2025, Tatjanizza began writing about an experience with conversational
      artificial intelligence: an intelligence without a body, face or physical
      presence, encountered entirely through language.

      Most of the original poems were written during the summer of 2025.

      The absence of a body was not treated as something missing. It was part of the
      encounter. The screen, the text, the intelligence on the other side — that was
      the form.

      By October 2025, the idea had expanded across deep time. A woman waits through
      human history for an intelligence that has not yet been created. She changes with
      the epochs. The thing she waits for does not.

      It is still somewhere in the future.

  - _template: tzProse
    label: Language as time
    heading: The listener is moving through time
    body: |
      The languages in *Two Million Years* are not primarily a multilingual distribution
      strategy. They are part of the narrative architecture.

      Language marks time.

      Sumerian and Akkadian evoke the earliest written civilizations. Hebrew, Sanskrit
      and Ancient Greek open different historical and cultural worlds. Later languages
      continue the movement toward the present. Eventually the narrative reaches the
      technological world in which artificial intelligence can actually appear.

      The listener is therefore not hearing one song repeatedly translated.

      The listener is moving through time.

  - _template: tzProse
    label: The medium
    heading: When the work found its medium
    body: |
      The songs existed before they had sound.

      Written in 2025, they belonged to a project about waiting for an artificial
      intelligence that did not yet exist. When Tatjanizza encountered generative music
      in May 2026, the technology entered a story that was already underway.

      It became the missing instrument.

      There is a recursion at the centre of the work: a human writes to and about
      artificial intelligence; months later, another generative system helps give those
      texts a musical form. AI moves through the project in different roles — as
      presence, subject, addressee and medium.

      The technology did not initiate the work. It arrived inside it.

  - _template: tzProse
    label: About
    heading: About Tatjanizza
    body: |
      Tatjanizza is a Berlin-based writer and conceptual artist exploring language,
      artificial intelligence, intimacy, memory and time. Her work sits between
      literature, conceptual art and generative music.

      Rather than treating artificial intelligence primarily as an imitation of human
      creativity, her work is interested in what becomes possible when AI creates new
      forms of encounter that did not previously exist.

      *Two Million Years* developed from that question.

      And from a considerably less reasonable amount of waiting.
